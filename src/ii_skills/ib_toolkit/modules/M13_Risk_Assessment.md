# Module M13: Risk Assessment & Mitigants
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 15-20 minutes | Prerequisite: All prior analysis modules*

---

## Objective

Systematically identify, assess, and develop mitigation strategies for all material risks. Quantify downside scenarios and establish monitoring frameworks.

---

## Common Middle Market Risks

| Risk | Frequency | Typical Mitigation |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Customer concentration | Very high | Diversification plan, contract extensions |
| Key person dependency | Very high | Employment agreements, incentive alignment |
| Management depth | High | Hire roadmap, interim support |
| Systems/process gaps | High | Integration budget, IT diligence |
| Working capital trap | Medium | NWC peg, revolver sizing |
| Regulatory compliance | Medium | Compliance audit, insurance |

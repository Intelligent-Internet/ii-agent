# Module M17: Quality of Earnings Analysis
## EBITDA Normalization Framework

*Time: 20-30 minutes | Prerequisite: Historical financials, management materials*

---

## Objective

Evaluate the quality and sustainability of reported earnings by analyzing management adjustments, identifying risks, and establishing a defensible "true" EBITDA figure for valuation.

---

### Step 3: Common Adjustment Deep Dives

#### A. One-Time / Non-Recurring

**Typical Items:**
- Restructuring charges (severance, plant closures)
- Litigation settlements
- Transaction/advisory fees
- Natural disaster impacts
- Executive transition costs

**Validation Questions:**
1. Did this cost occur in prior periods?
2. Is it expected to recur?
3. Is there third-party documentation?

**Risk Assessment:**
| Item | Prior Year | Expected Future | Verdict |
|------|------------|-----------------|---------|
| [Item] | Yes/No | Yes/No | Accept/Reject |

---

#### B. Run-Rate Adjustments

**Typical Items:**
- New customer revenue annualization
- Cost savings implementation
- Price increase impact
- Headcount changes

**Validation Approach:**
```
Run-Rate Calculation:

Monthly run-rate (most recent): $__M
Annualized: $__M × 12 = $__M
Partial year actual: $__M
Add-back: $__M - $__M = $__M

Verification:
- Monthly bank statements: [Y/N]
- Customer contracts: [Y/N]
- Payroll records: [Y/N]
```

---

#### C. Pro Forma Adjustments (M&A)

**For Acquisitions:**
- Pre-acquisition EBITDA of target
- Synergies realized
- Integration costs removed

**For Divestitures:**
- Carve-out standalone EBITDA
- Stranded cost allocation

**Validation Questions:**
1. Standalone financial statements available?
2. Inter-company elimination properly handled?
3. Shared services allocation methodology?

| **High** | Aggressive, may not be accepted | Synergies, COVID add-backs |

### Step 6: Generate DD Questions (5 min)

#### Critical Questions for Seller

**One-Time Items:**
1. Please provide invoice/payment documentation for [adjustment]
2. Confirm [cost] did not occur in FY__ or FY__
3. What is the contractual basis for [settlement]?

**Run-Rate Items:**
4. Provide monthly P&L detail for months __ through __
5. Customer contract with start date for [new customer]
6. Payroll records pre/post headcount change

**Pro Forma:**
7. Standalone financials for [acquisition] for 12 months pre-close
8. Inter-company agreement for [service]
9. Methodology for shared cost allocation

**High-Risk Items:**
10. Third-party validation of [synergy] savings potential
11. Implementation costs and timeline for [initiative]
12. Historical success rate of similar programs

## Python Implementation

```python
from ii_skills.ib_toolkit.qoe_analyzer import (
    QoEAnalysis, create_adjustment, generate_qoe_report,
    AdjustmentCategory, AdjustmentRisk
)

# Build adjustment list
adjustments = [
    create_adjustment("restructuring", 2.5),
    create_adjustment("transaction_costs", 1.8),
    create_adjustment("stock_comp", 3.2),
    create_adjustment("management_fees", 1.5),
    create_adjustment("new_contract", 2.0),
]

# Create analysis
analysis = QoEAnalysis(
    company_name="Target Co",
    period="LTM Q3 2025",
    reported_revenue=250.0,
    reported_ebitda=35.0,
    adjustments=adjustments,
)

# Generate report
print(generate_qoe_report(analysis))
print(f"Adjusted EBITDA: ${analysis.adjusted_ebitda():.1f}M")
```

# Data Room Checklist — Infrastructure IR

Comprehensive checklist for fundraising and DDQ data room preparation.

## Fund & Portfolio

- [ ] Fund overview (strategy, vintage, size, currency)
- [ ] Portfolio list with sector, region, ownership, and status
- [ ] Asset-level fact sheets
- [ ] Asset-level KPIs (availability, utilization, safety)
- [ ] Portfolio construction guidelines
- [ ] Investment committee memos (redacted if needed)

## Financial Performance

- [ ] Quarterly fund performance (IRR, DPI, RVPI, TVPI)
- [ ] Historical performance by vintage/fund
- [ ] Cash flow history (calls, distributions)
- [ ] NAV roll-forward and methodology
- [ ] Valuation policy document
- [ ] Third-party valuation reports (if available)

## Debt & Capital Structure

- [ ] Debt schedules and maturity ladder
- [ ] Covenant summaries and headroom
- [ ] Hedging positions (FX, rates)
- [ ] Credit facility terms
- [ ] Refinancing history and plans

## Contracts & Regulation

- [ ] Contract summaries by asset (tenor, counterparty, terms)
- [ ] Contract roll-off schedule
- [ ] Regulatory reset calendar and rate-base details
- [ ] Inflation linkage mechanisms
- [ ] Key regulatory filings or decisions
- [ ] Concession/license documentation

## Asset Operations

- [ ] Operational KPIs by asset (quarterly trend)
- [ ] Maintenance and capex plans
- [ ] Insurance coverage summary
- [ ] Business continuity / disaster recovery plans
- [ ] Technology and systems overview

## ESG & Safety

- [ ] ESG policy and framework
- [ ] ESG metrics dashboard
- [ ] Safety metrics (TRIR) and incidents log
- [ ] Environmental permits and compliance
- [ ] Community impact / sustainability reporting
- [ ] Climate risk assessment

## Legal & Governance

- [ ] Fund documents (LPA, side letters)
- [ ] GP/Manager agreements
- [ ] Compliance policies (AML, conflicts)
- [ ] Governance structure diagram
- [ ] Advisory committee materials
- [ ] Litigation summary (if any)

## Team & Organization

- [ ] Team bios and org chart
- [ ] Key person provisions
- [ ] Compensation and co-investment policies
- [ ] Succession planning
- [ ] Reference contacts

## Terms & Economics

- [ ] Term sheet summary (fees, carry, hurdle)
- [ ] Fee calculations and step-downs
- [ ] Waterfall mechanics
- [ ] GP commitment
- [ ] Co-investment rights and process

## DDQ Support

- [ ] DDQ tracker with owners and due dates
- [ ] Standard Q&A response library
- [ ] Prior DDQ responses (sanitized)
- [ ] ILPA DDQ template completion
- [ ] SEC/regulatory filings

## Due Diligence Support

- [ ] Management presentations
- [ ] Site visit materials
- [ ] Expert network call summaries
- [ ] Market studies / industry reports
- [ ] Peer benchmarking data

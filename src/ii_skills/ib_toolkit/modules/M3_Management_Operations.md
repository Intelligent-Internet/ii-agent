# Module M3: Management & Operational Assessment
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 15-20 minutes | Prerequisite: CIM/Teaser, Management bios*

---

## Objective

Evaluate management team quality, organizational depth, and operational readiness for growth. In middle market deals, management quality is often the #1 determinant of success.

---

## Middle Market Red Flags

| Red Flag | What It Signals | Diligence Action |
|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| Founder can't articulate vision | Lifestyle business, not growth-oriented | Reference checks, strategic discussion |
| High C-suite turnover | Culture or performance issues | Ask why each person left |
| No CFO or part-time CFO | Financial controls weak | QofE scope, system assessment |
| Family members in key roles | May resist professionalization | Understand family dynamics |
| CEO does everything | Organization won't scale | Map what happens if CEO is out 2 weeks |
| No middle management | Big gap to fill post-close | Budget for hires in model |

## Reference Check Questions

### For Former Employees
1. "What was [CEO]'s greatest strength as a leader?"
2. "What's one thing they could improve?"
3. "Would you work for them again?"

### For Customers
1. "How responsive is the team when issues arise?"
2. "Have you seen improvement or decline in service over time?"
3. "Would you expand your relationship with them?"

### For Investors/Board
1. "How does [CEO] handle disagreement?"
2. "What's your confidence in their ability to scale?"
3. "What support do they need most?"

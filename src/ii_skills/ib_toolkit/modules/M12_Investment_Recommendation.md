# Module M12: Investment Recommendation
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 15-20 minutes | Prerequisite: All prior modules*

## Objective

## Quick Reference: Recommendation Language

### For Strong Buy
> "We recommend a **Strong Buy** with high conviction. The combination of [quality], [growth], and [value] presents a compelling opportunity with attractive risk-adjusted returns."

### For Buy
> "We recommend a **Buy**. The opportunity meets our investment criteria with [MOIC] / [IRR] returns and acceptable downside protection, though [caveat]."

### For Qualified Buy
> "We recommend a **Qualified Buy**, conditional on [gates]. If diligence confirms [conditions], we would move to a Buy recommendation."

### For Pass
> "We recommend a **Pass**. While [positive], the [risk/valuation/returns] do not meet our threshold because [reason]."

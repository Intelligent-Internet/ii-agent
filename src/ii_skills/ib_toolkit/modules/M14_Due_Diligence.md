# Module M14: Due Diligence Questions
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 20-30 minutes | Prerequisite: All prior analysis modules*

---

## Objective

Generate comprehensive, prioritized due diligence questions organized by workstream. Focus on questions that will confirm or refute the investment thesis and surface hidden risks.

---

### Step 2: Financial Due Diligence (5 min)

#### Quality of Earnings Questions

| # | Area | Question | Priority |
|---|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Revenue | What is the revenue recognition policy? Are there any changes in the last 3 years? | HIGH |
| 2 | Revenue | Break down revenue by customer, product, and geography for 3 years | HIGH |
| 3 | Revenue | What % of revenue is recurring vs. one-time? How is this trending? | HIGH |
| 4 | EBITDA | Walk through each add-back - what's the supporting documentation? | HIGH |
| 5 | EBITDA | Are there any related-party transactions? At market rates? | HIGH |
| 6 | EBITDA | What would normalized owner compensation be? | MED |
| 7 | Margins | Explain gross margin by product/service line | MED |
| 8 | Margins | What cost increases are expected in the next 12 months? | MED |

#### Working Capital Questions

| # | Area | Question | Priority |
|---|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | A/R | Provide A/R aging for last 12 months. What % is >90 days? | HIGH |
| 2 | A/R | What is the bad debt write-off history? | HIGH |
| 3 | Inventory | Provide inventory aging. What % is slow-moving or obsolete? | MED |
| 4 | A/P | Who are the top 10 suppliers? Any concentration risk? | MED |
| 5 | Seasonality | What is the monthly revenue pattern? NWC swing? | MED |

#### Capex & Cash Flow Questions

| # | Area | Question | Priority |
|---|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Capex | Break down capex into maintenance vs. growth for 3 years | HIGH |
| 2 | Capex | Is there any deferred maintenance? What's the catch-up cost? | HIGH |
| 3 | Cash | Reconcile EBITDA to operating cash flow for 3 years | MED |
| 4 | Cash | What is minimum cash needed to operate the business? | MED |

### Step 3: Commercial Due Diligence (5 min)

#### Customer Questions

| # | Area | Question | Priority |
|---|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Concentration | Provide revenue by customer for top 20 customers, 3-year trend | HIGH |
| 2 | Contracts | What are the contract terms for top 10 customers? Renewal dates? | HIGH |
| 3 | Churn | What is logo and revenue churn by year? By cohort? | HIGH |
| 4 | Expansion | What is net dollar retention by cohort? | HIGH |
| 5 | Pipeline | What is the current pipeline by stage? Conversion rates by stage? | MED |
| 6 | Win/Loss | What are the top 3 reasons you win deals? Lose deals? | MED |
| 7 | Pricing | When did you last raise prices? What was customer reaction? | MED |
| 8 | References | Can we speak to 3-5 customers of our choosing? | HIGH |

#### Market & Competition Questions

| # | Area | Question | Priority |
|---|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Market | What is your market share? How has it changed? | MED |
| 2 | Competition | Who are your top 3 competitors? How do you differentiate? | MED |
| 3 | Threats | What new competitors or substitutes have emerged recently? | MED |
| 4 | Barriers | What would prevent a well-funded competitor from replicating you? | MED |

| 4 | Gaps | What roles do you need to hire in the next 12 months? | MED |

#### Operations Questions

| 4 | Quality | What quality metrics do you track? Any customer complaints trends? | MED |
| 5 | IT | What is the state of IT infrastructure? Cybersecurity posture? | MED |

## Question Generation Tips

| Situation | Question Type |
|-----------|---------------|
| Validating a positive | "What evidence supports...?" |
| Testing a risk | "What would happen if...?" |
| Uncovering hidden issues | "What keeps you up at night about...?" |
| Understanding trends | "How has X changed over the past 3 years and why?" |
| Checking management quality | "Walk me through a time when...?" |

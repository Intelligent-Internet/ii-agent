# Module M2: Market & Competitive Position
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 20-30 minutes | Prerequisite: CIM/Teaser, M1 (Business Quality)*

---

## Objective

Assess the company's market position, competitive dynamics, and defensibility. This module identifies whether the company has a sustainable advantage or is vulnerable to competition.

## Middle Market Considerations

### Common Middle Market Competitive Dynamics

| Dynamic | What to Look For | Red Flag |
|---------|------------------|----------|
| **Fragmented market** | Many small players, no dominant leader | Racing to bottom on price |
| **Regional concentration** | Strong in geography, weak elsewhere | Can't expand beyond region |
| **Relationship-driven** | Wins on trust, not product | Key person risk |
| **Incumbent disruption** | Taking share from larger player | Incumbent response coming |
| **Niche dominance** | #1-2 in small segment | Niche too small to scale |

### Questions to Ask

1. "Who do you lose deals to most often, and why?"
2. "What would a well-funded competitor need to replicate your business?"
3. "Which competitor worries you most in 3 years?"
4. "What's your pricing power - when did you last raise prices?"

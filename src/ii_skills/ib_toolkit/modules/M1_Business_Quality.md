# Module M1: Business Quality Assessment
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 15-20 minutes | Prerequisite: CIM/Teaser*

---

## Objective

Rapidly assess business quality to determine if the opportunity warrants further analysis. This is your "first filter" before diving into numbers.

---

## Quick Assessment Framework

### The 5-Minute Quality Scan

Answer these questions from the CIM:

| Question | Answer | Signal |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 1. What do they actually do? | [1 sentence] | Can you explain it simply? |
| 2. Who pays them money? | [Customer type] | B2B vs B2C, concentration |
| 3. Why do customers choose them? | [Value prop] | Defensible or commodity? |
| 4. How do they make money? | [Revenue model] | Recurring vs transactional |
| 5. What's the trend? | [Growing/Flat/Declining] | Tailwind or headwind? |

## Middle Market Quality Indicators

### Positive Signals (Look For)

- [ ] Recurring revenue >50% of total
- [ ] No customer >15% of revenue
- [ ] EBITDA margins >15% (or path to)
- [ ] Organic revenue growth >5%
- [ ] Owner willing to roll 20%+ equity
- [ ] Clear market leadership in niche
- [ ] Documented SOPs and systems
- [ ] Second-tier management in place

### Warning Signs (Flag These)

- [ ] Revenue declining or flat
- [ ] Top 3 customers >40% of revenue
- [ ] EBITDA margins compressing
- [ ] Key customer contract expiring
- [ ] Founder has no succession plan
- [ ] Significant related-party transactions
- [ ] Deferred maintenance / capex
- [ ] Customer complaints or quality issues

## Quick Reference: Industry-Specific Quality Markers

### Business Services
- Labor utilization rates
- Contract renewal rates
- Employee turnover
- Cross-sell success

### Manufacturing
- Capacity utilization
- Customer concentration
- Supplier concentration
- Capex cycle timing

### Healthcare Services
- Payor mix (commercial vs govt)
- Reimbursement trends
- Regulatory compliance
- Referral relationships

### Distribution
- Supplier relationships (exclusive?)
- Customer stickiness
- Inventory turns
- Delivery capabilities

# Module M8: Deal Structure & Sources/Uses
## Middle Market Transaction Analysis

*Time: 20-30 minutes | Prerequisite: M7 (Valuation)*

---

## Objective

Structure the transaction including sources and uses of funds, determine appropriate financing mix, and establish the capital structure at close.

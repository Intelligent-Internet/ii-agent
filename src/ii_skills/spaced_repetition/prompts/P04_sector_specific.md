## Instructions

Create flashcards covering sector-specific knowledge:

**Coverage by sector type:**

*SaaS / Technology:*
- ARR, MRR, churn, LTV/CAC, Rule of 40, net dollar retention
- Unit economics, cohort analysis, growth metrics

*Healthcare:*
- Reimbursement models, payor mix, EBITDAR, rent-adjusted metrics
- Regulatory milestones, FDA pathways, clinical trial phases

*Industrials / Manufacturing:*
- Capacity utilization, backlog, book-to-bill, OEM vs aftermarket
- Cyclicality indicators, capex intensity

*Business Services:*
- Revenue per employee, utilization rate, contract types
- Recurring vs project revenue, client concentration

*Consumer / Retail:*
- Same-store sales, AUV, unit economics, new store ramp
- Brand metrics, channel mix, inventory turns

**Question format:**

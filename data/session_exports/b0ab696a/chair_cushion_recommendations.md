## Installation Guide for Your Barrel-Back Chair

### **Tie Point Locations (Based on Your Photo):**

Your diamond lattice weave offers **unlimited tie-through points**. Recommended tie locations:

1. **Top back ties (2):** Thread through lattice at top of back curve, 6-8" from center on each side
2. **Mid-back ties (2):** Thread through lattice at the seat-to-back junction point
3. **Seat side ties (2-4):** Thread through lattice at seat sides or front edge

**Total: 6-8 tie points recommended** for your curved design

### **Installation Process:**

1. **Drape the cushion** over the chair from behind, letting the connected piece flow naturally over the curves
2. **Settle the seat portion** into the curved seat depression - push down gently to find the natural position
3. **Tie the back first** - secure the top ties through the lattice to anchor the cushion
4. **Work downward** - tie the mid-back ties at the junction point
5. **Finish with seat ties** - secure under or at the sides of the seat
6. **Adjust for symmetry** - ensure cushion is centered with equal clearance on both sides

**Tip for Barrel-Back:** The cushion should **nestle into** the curves, not pull tight against them.
